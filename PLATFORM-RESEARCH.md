# Platform research: Shopify

## Status
Research/partner-feasibility workspace only. WiPay's Shopify page points merchants to Payphora, a third-party bridge; that is not proof of a WiPay-built or WiPay-supported Shopify gateway.

## Platform constraint
A Shopify payment gateway app must use Shopify's Payments API and obtain Shopify authorization. This differs from an ordinary app wrapping WiPay hosted checkout. Do not start a public native gateway until WiPay confirms it will pursue partner authorization and operational obligations.

## Next decisions
- Verify Payphora ownership, WiPay support, regions, fees, privacy/security, and order reconciliation.
- Ask WiPay whether it will apply for Shopify Payments Partner access.
- Confirm approved territories and currencies.
